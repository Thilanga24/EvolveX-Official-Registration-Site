What is EvolveX?

EvolveX 2.0 is a next-era esports initiative organized by the Rotaract Club of University of Kelaniya. It is designed to go beyond traditional gaming tournaments by creating a complete esports ecosystem that connects players, developers, and industry experts under one platform.

EvolveX is not just a competition. Not just an experience. It is a movement that empowers youth through esports, innovation, and knowledge sharing.

Three Pillars – One Revolution

Experience the Full Spectrum of EvolveX 2.0

Gaming Competition

The ultimate battleground for champions. Compete with the best teams, showcase your skills, and rise through Sri Lanka’s competitive gaming ranks.

Game Development Competition

Innovate and build the future. Participate in game development challenges, workshops, and sessions that encourage creativity and technical skill.

Gaming Competition – The Battleground

EvolveX 2.0 hosts an intense esports competition where top gamers and teams compete for victory. This is your chance to represent your university, prove your skill, and become a champion in Sri Lanka’s evolving esports scene.

  • Team based matches
  • Professional tournament structure
  • Exciting prizes and recognition
  • Live audience & streaming

Game Development Competition & Workshops

EvolveX encourages not only players but creators. Through game development competitions and knowledge sessions, participants can explore how games are built, designed, and optimized.

This is a space for innovators, programmers, designers, and creative thinkers to shine.

Esports & Gaming Conference
